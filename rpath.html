Rpath Suitability Assessment

The University of the Sunshine Coast offers some students the opportunity to complete a special research project under an academic supervisor as a part of their undergraduate. The project runs over a 10 week period with approximately 100 hours of dedicated study on a topic co-designed between the student and supervisor.

IMAGE PLACEHOLDER

Project Overview:

The goal of the project was to investigate Rpath, an R package that uses bioenergetics to model energy flows within ecosystems, for its suitability at modelling marine ecosystems. A preliminary investigation of the package's capabilities was conducted to determine suitability for a future research project investigating how perturbations from several climate change scenarios would impact a model marine ecosystem.

Key Objectives:

  • Describe and showcase Rpath's capabilities
  • Investigate resources for learning and teaching Rpath
  • Determine what data is required for application
  • Identify potential data sources
  • Identify key barriers and limitations of the package

Project Outcomes:

The project allowed me to develop skills in independent research. While investigating Rpath I had to take deliberate care in my note-taking and time management to accurately present information weekly to my supervisor.
